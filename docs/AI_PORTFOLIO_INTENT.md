This branch is dedicated to transforming my AI master’s thesis into an
industry-presentable research engineering portfolio.

- Reviewers who skim first and look for system thinking.

Constraints:

- Systems > prose.
- No marketing language.
- No premature optimization or over-engineering.

Non-goals (for now):
- Visual redesign
- Performance tuning
- Accessibility overhaul
- Design system refactors
